Ayati Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · العربية

Ayati is an offline Android Quran reader with optional daily reminders, provided by Anas Fikri (Gwhyyy). This policy covers com.gwhyyy.ayat_notifications.

Information stored by the app

The Arabic Quran and English translation are bundled with the app. Your saved reading position, reminder time, language and appearance are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.

Collection and sharing

Ayati does not send this information to us. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, remote crash-reporting service, or backend. The release app does not request internet access. We do not collect, sell, or share your reading data.

Android may back up or restore app data according to your device and Google account settings. These operating-system services are controlled by their providers. Links opened in a browser are subject to the destination website's privacy policy.

Android permissions and reminders

Notification permission displays your chosen daily reminder. Alarm access schedules it at the selected time. Startup, vibration and wake-lock permissions support restoring and delivering local notifications. Denied permissions or device power restrictions can prevent delivery.

Ayati prepares a rolling 30-day reminder schedule and refreshes it when you open the app. Reopen the app regularly to keep future reminders scheduled. It does not use a remote background service.

Retention and deletion

Your preferences and saved position stay in local app storage until that storage is cleared. You can remove local data through Android settings or by uninstalling Ayati. Manage operating-system backups separately in your device or account settings. There is no Ayati account to delete.

Text sources

Arabic Quran text: Tanzil Uthmani text and license. Translation by Talal Itani, ClearQuran.com, distributed under CC BY-ND 4.0 and the publisher's format-conversion permission.

Children, contact and changes

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through the app. For privacy questions, email work@gwhyyy.com. Updates will appear here with a revised effective date.

الأذونات والتذكيرات

يُستخدم إذن الإشعارات لعرض التذكير، وإذن المنبهات لجدولته في الوقت المحدد، وأذونات بدء التشغيل وإبقاء الجهاز مستيقظاً لدعم استعادة الإشعارات المحلية وإيصالها. قد يمنع رفض الأذونات أو تقييد البطارية وصول التذكيرات.

يجهّز التطبيق جدول تذكيرات لمدة 30 يوماً ويجدده عند فتحه. افتح التطبيق بانتظام لاستمرار جدولة التذكيرات القادمة. لا يستخدم التطبيق خدمة خلفية عن بُعد.
