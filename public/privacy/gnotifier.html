GNotifier Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

GNotifier is an Android reminder app provided by Anas Fikri (Gwhyyy). This policy covers com.gwhyyy.gnotifier.

Information stored by the app

GNotifier stores your reminders, titles, notes, chosen dates and times, daily repeat choices, saved reminders, and notification preferences in its private storage on your phone. It also stores the display name you enter during setup. Shared text is stored only when you save it as a reminder.

Collection and sharing

The app does not send this information to us. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, remote crash-reporting service, or backend. The release app does not request internet access. We do not collect, sell, or share your reminder data.

Android may back up or restore app data according to your device and Google account settings. These operating-system services are controlled by their providers. Links you open, or text you choose to share with another app, are handled by that app under its own privacy policy.

If you choose Contact, GNotifier opens your email app with your display name in the subject. Only send information you want to share. We receive the message and sender details if you send it, and use them to respond to your request.

Android permissions

  • Notifications: displays reminders you create.
  • Alarms and reminders: schedules notifications at your chosen time.
  • Run after startup: restores scheduled reminders after a restart.
  • Vibration and wake lock: supports notification delivery and vibration when enabled.

You control permissions in Android settings. Denying notification or alarm access can prevent reminders from being scheduled or displayed. Device power restrictions can also affect delivery.

Retention and deletion

Reminders stay in local app storage until you delete them or use the app's deletion controls. If you enable automatic deletion for a reminder, opening its notification removes the stored reminder. You can remove local app data through Android settings or by uninstalling GNotifier. Manage any operating-system backups separately in your device or account settings. There is no GNotifier account to delete.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through the app.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email work@gwhyyy.com. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date.
